Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a transaction?

Open the Transactions tab and tap the "+" button. Choose the type (buy, sell, etc.), enter the details, and save.

Where is my data stored?

All your portfolio, transaction, and watchlist data is stored locally on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app will remove this information.

How do I export my data?

You can export your transactions and portfolio from the Settings screen as a CSV file.

Where does market data come from?

CoinhakoZen fetches public market data from reputable third-party providers to display prices, charts, and analytics.

How do I delete my data?

You can clear all local data by uninstalling the app, or by using the reset option in Settings.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your portfolio data is not transmitted to our servers. Please see the Privacy Policy for full details.
