Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CoinhakoZen ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: CoinhakoZen is designed with privacy in mind. Your portfolio data is stored locally on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

CoinhakoZen does not require account registration. Any transactions, holdings, or watchlist entries you create are stored locally on your device.

1.2 Automatically collected information

We may collect limited, anonymous technical information such as app version, device type, and crash diagnostics to improve stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain core app functionality.
  • To deliver market data and pricing information.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the app.

3. Market Data

CoinhakoZen fetches public market data from third-party providers to display prices, charts, and analytics. These providers may receive standard technical request information (such as IP address) according to their own privacy policies.

4. Local Data Storage

Your portfolio, transactions, and watchlist data are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. We do not transmit your personal portfolio data to our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party services for market data and analytics. These services have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

6. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoZen is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use the app responsibly and protect access to your device.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Because most data is stored locally on your device, you can manage this directly within the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app and on this page, with an updated "Last updated" date.
